Question Link : https://leetcode.com/problems/accounts-merge/description/

* Core intuition:
  Treat every account as a node and connect two accounts whenever they share an email.

* Why DSU:
  Shared emails create connected components. If A shares an email with B and B shares another email with C, all three accounts belong to the same merged account.

* mailIdToAccountIdMap:
  Stores the account where each email was first encountered. When the same email appears again, merge the two accounts.

* parents:
  DSU structure where each account initially belongs to its own component.

* merge():
  Finds the roots of both accounts and connects one root to the other.

* getRootNode():
  Finds the representative of an account's connected component.

* Path compression:
  After finding the root, directly connect the current node to the root to make future DSU operations faster.

* mergedAccountMap:
  After all merges are complete, find the root of every email's account and group emails by that root.

* Why grouping happens after all merges:
  An account's final component may change through a chain of shared emails, so grouping before completing all unions could put emails under the wrong component.

* Why sort emails:
  The problem requires emails within each merged account to be lexicographically sorted.

* Account name:
  Any account in the same connected component represents the same person, so using the root account's name is sufficient.

* Time Complexity: O(E log E + E α(A))

* Space Complexity: O(A + E)
