Question Link : https://leetcode.com/problems/minimize-the-total-price-of-the-trips/description/

*   Every trip travels
        along the unique path
        between its start and end nodes.

*   First objective:

        determine how many times
        every node is used
        across all trips.

*   WHY?

        Every visit contributes

            price[node]

        to the final answer.

*   Therefore,

        total contribution of a node is

            price[node]
            ×
            frequency[node]

*   DFS is used
        for every trip
        to identify its unique path.

*   Once frequencies are known,

        trips no longer matter.

*   The problem becomes:

        Choose some nodes
        whose prices will be halved.

*   Constraint:

        Adjacent nodes
        cannot both be halved.

*   This becomes
        the classic Tree DP
        similar to House Robber III.

*   DP State:

        dp(node, parentHalfed)

*   where

        parentHalfed = 0

            Parent was NOT halved.

            Current node
            may either

                halve itself

                OR

                keep full price.

*   parentHalfed = 1

            Parent was halved.

            Therefore current node
            cannot be halved.

*   At every node
        evaluate two choices.

*   Choice 1:

        Keep full price.

*   Cost becomes:

        current full contribution

        +

        best answer
        from every child.

*   Choice 2:

        Halve current node.

*   Possible only when

        parent is not halved.

*   Current contribution becomes

        half of its total cost.

*   Since current node
        is halved,

        every child
        is forced
        to keep full price.

*   Transition:

        answer =

        minimum(

            keep full price,

            halve current node

        )

*   Memoization stores
        every Tree DP state
        only once.

*   Time Complexity:

        Frequency computation:

            O(trips × n)

        Tree DP:

            O(n)

*   Overall Time Complexity:

        O(trips × n)

*   Space Complexity:

        O(n)
