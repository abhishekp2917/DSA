Question Link : https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock-with-transaction-fee/description/

*   We may perform unlimited transactions.

*   Every completed transaction requires paying a fixed transaction fee.

*   In this implementation, the fee is charged during BUY instead of SELL.

*   Charging the fee during BUY or SELL is mathematically equivalent because every transaction contains exactly one buy and one sell.

*   Therefore, the state remains identical to the unlimited transactions problem.

*   DP State:

        dp[day][hasStock]

*   If we currently own a stock, we have two choices:

        Sell today

        OR

        Continue holding.

*   Selling simply adds today's stock price because the fee has already been paid while buying.

*   If we do not own a stock, we again have two choices:

        Buy today

        OR

        Skip today.

*   Buying subtracts both the stock price and the transaction fee from the current profit.

*   Memoization computes every (day, state) only once.

*   Tabulation processes trading days from left to right while maintaining the best profit for both holding states.

*   The final answer is dp[n][0] because any stock still being held has not yet been converted into realised profit.

*   Time Complexity:
        O(n)

*   Space Complexity:
        O(n)
