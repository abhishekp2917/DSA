Question Link : https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock-ii/description/

*   We may perform unlimited transactions.

*   At any time, we can either hold at most one stock or hold none.

*   Therefore, the only information needed to describe a state is:

        Current day

        Whether we currently own a stock

*   DP State:

        dp[day][hasStock]

*   If we already own a stock, we have only two choices:

        Sell today

        OR

        Continue holding it.

*   If we do not own a stock, we again have only two choices:

        Buy today

        OR

        Skip today.

*   Every decision moves to the next day, so the problem naturally forms a DP.

*   Memoization computes every (day, state) only once.

*   Tabulation stores:

        dp[day][0] = maximum profit after day while not holding a stock.

        dp[day][1] = maximum profit after day while holding one stock.

*   Holding state can come from: Continuing to hold OR Buying today.

*   Not holding state can come from: Continuing without a stock OR Selling today.

*   The final answer is dp[n][0] because any stock still being held has not yet been converted into realised profit.

*   Time Complexity:
        O(n)

*   Space Complexity:
        O(n)
