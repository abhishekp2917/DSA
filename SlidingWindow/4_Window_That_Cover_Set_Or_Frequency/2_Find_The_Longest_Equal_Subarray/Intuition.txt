Question Link : https://leetcode.com/problems/find-the-longest-equal-subarray/description/

*   We may delete at most k elements from the array.

*   After deleting those elements, the remaining equal elements become adjacent.

*   Instead of directly deleting elements, maintain a sliding window.

*   Inside every window, choose one value to keep.

*   Every other value inside the window must be deleted.

*   Therefore: Required deletions = Window size - Maximum frequency inside the window

*   If the required deletions exceed k, the window is no longer valid.

*   Shrink the window until it becomes valid again.

*   Maintain the frequency of every number inside the current window.

*   Also maintain the maximum frequency inside the window.

*   The answer is the largest frequency found in any valid window.

*   Time Complexity:

        O(n)

*   Space Complexity:

        O(n)
