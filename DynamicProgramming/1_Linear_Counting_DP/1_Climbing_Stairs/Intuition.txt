Question Link : https://leetcode.com/problems/climbing-stairs/

*   We need to count the number of distinct ways to reach the nth stair.

*   At every move, we can climb either:
        1 step
        or
        2 steps

*   Key observation:
        The last move completely determines where we came from.

*   To reach stair n:

        either the last move was 1 step
        coming from stair (n-1)

        or the last move was 2 steps
        coming from stair (n-2)

*   Therefore:

        ways(n) =
            ways(n-1) + ways(n-2)

*   Base Cases:

        ways(0) = 1

    because there is exactly one way
        to stay at the starting position.

        ways(1) = 1

    because only one single-step move is possible.

*   Memoization:
        stores answers for previously solved stairs,
        avoiding repeated recursion.

*   Tabulation:
        computes answers from smaller stairs
        towards larger stairs.

*   Every state depends only on
        the previous two states.

*   This recurrence is identical to
        the Fibonacci sequence.

*   Time Complexity:

        Memoization:
            O(n)

        Tabulation:
            O(n)

*   Space Complexity:

        Memoization:
            O(n)

        Tabulation:
            O(n)

    (Can be optimized to O(1)
     since only previous two states are required.)
