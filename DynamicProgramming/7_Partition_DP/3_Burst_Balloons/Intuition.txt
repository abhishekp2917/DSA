Question Link : https://leetcode.com/problems/burst-balloons/description/

*   We need to maximize the total coins obtained after bursting every balloon.

*   Bursting balloon i earns:

        left × nums[i] × right

*   The neighbours keep changing after every burst.

*   Choosing the first balloon is difficult because future neighbours are unknown.

*   Instead, think in reverse.

*   Assume one balloon is burst LAST inside every interval.

*   When balloon i is the last remaining balloon in an interval, all other balloons inside that interval have already disappeared.

*   Therefore, its neighbours become the balloons immediately outside the interval.

*   This makes the left and right intervals completely independent.

*   DP State:

        dp[start][end]

    stores the maximum coins obtainable by bursting every balloon inside interval [start,end].

*   Base case:

        start > end

*   No balloons remain.

*   Therefore the answer is 0.

*   Try every balloon as the last balloon.

*   Coins earned are:

        left neighbour × current balloon × right neighbour

*   Plus the optimal answer of the left interval.

*   Plus the optimal answer of the right interval.

*   Transition:

        dp[start][end]

        =

        maximum over every last balloon

        of

        current coins

        +

        left interval

        +

        right interval

*   Memoization computes every interval only once.

*   Tabulation builds smaller intervals before larger intervals.

*   Number of DP states:

        O(n²)

*   Every state tries every possible last balloon.

*   Time Complexity:
        O(n³)

*   Space Complexity:
        O(n²)
