Question Link : https://leetcode.com/problems/last-stone-weight-ii/description/


*   We need to minimize
        the final remaining stone weight.

*   Key observation:
        The order of smashing stones
        does not matter.

*   WHY?

        Every smash operation
        effectively places one stone
        into one group

        and the other stone
        into another group.

*   Eventually,
        every stone belongs
        to one of two groups.

*   Let:

        Group1 weight = S

        Group2 weight = TotalWeight - S

*   Final remaining weight becomes:

        |Group1 - Group2|

        =

        |TotalWeight - 2 × S|

*   Therefore the problem becomes:

        Partition the stones
        into two groups

        such that

        their weight difference
        is minimum.

*   DP State:

        dp[i][sum]

    stores the minimum possible difference
        after processing stones
        from index i onward,

        when the first group
        currently has weight = sum.

*   At every stone
        there are exactly two choices:

        Put it into Group 1

        OR

        Put it into Group 2

*   If placed into Group 1,

        current sum increases.

*   If placed into Group 2,

        current sum remains unchanged.

*   At the end,

        Group 2's weight is

            TotalWeight - sum

    so the final answer becomes

        |TotalWeight - 2 × sum|

*   Transition:

        answer =

        min(

            include,

            exclude

        )

*   Memoization:
        stores answers for previously solved states,
        avoiding repeated recursion.

*   Tabulation:
        computes states
        from the last stone
        towards the first stone.

*   Time Complexity:

        O(n × TotalWeight)

*   Space Complexity:

        Memoization:
            O(n × TotalWeight)

        Tabulation:
            O(n × TotalWeight)

    (Can be optimized further
     using subset-sum DP.)
