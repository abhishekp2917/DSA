* Core intuition:
  Maintain a Trie with two counters at every character node: endCount for exact words and prefixCount for words passing through that prefix.

* insert():
  Traverse/create the word's path and increment prefixCount at every character node because the inserted word contributes to every one of its prefixes.

* endCount:
  Increment only at the final node because it represents the number of occurrences of the exact word.

* countWordsEqualTo():
  Traverse the complete word and return endCount of the final node.

* countWordsStartingWith():
  Traverse the prefix and return prefixCount of the final node because every word having this prefix passes through that node.

* erase():
  First traverse the complete path and store its nodes because prefixCount must be decremented only after confirming that the exact word exists.

* Why check endCount:
  The Trie path can exist even when the exact word was never inserted, for example when a longer word has the given word as its prefix.

* After confirming the word exists:
  Decrement endCount at the final node and prefixCount at every node on the word's path.

* Nodes are not physically deleted:
  They can remain in the Trie with prefixCount = 0 because future insertions may reuse the same path.

* Duplicate words:
  endCount allows the same word to be inserted multiple times and erased one occurrence at a time.

* Time Complexity: O(L)

* Space Complexity: O(total characters inserted)
