Question Link : https://leetcode.com/problems/sum-of-prefix-scores-of-strings/description/

* Core intuition:
  Build a Trie where every node stores how many words share the prefix represented by that node.

* First pass:
  Insert every word and increment count at every node along its path.

* Why count:
  The count of a prefix is exactly the number of words containing that prefix, which is the score required by the problem.

* Example:
  For ["abc", "ab", "bc"], the node representing "a" has count = 2 and the node representing "ab" has count = 2.

* Second pass:
  Traverse each word again and add the count of every prefix node to that word's answer.

* Why two passes:
  All prefix counts must be known before calculating the score of any word.

* Time Complexity: O(total characters)

* Space Complexity: O(total characters)
